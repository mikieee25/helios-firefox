First-use consent

Send downloads to Helios

When you send or intercept a download, this extension sends its URL, referrer, file name, and the fact that a download was requested to the Helios desktop app at 127.0.0.1 on this computer. This lets Helios identify the requested file.

Cookies and browser details are sent only if you separately enable them and grant Firefox’s optional website content permission. They let Helios download files that need your login. Nothing is sent to the developer or a remote server, and the extension has no analytics. Interception remains off until you accept and turn it on in the extension settings.
