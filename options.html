Helios integration

Before you enable interception

For a download, this extension sends the URL, referrer, file name, and the fact a download was requested to the Helios desktop app at 127.0.0.1 on this computer. Cookies and browser details are sent only if you separately enable them and grant Firefox's optional website content permission.

Helios listens on this computer only. Nothing is sent to the developer or a remote server, and the extension has no analytics. You can keep interception off or change these settings later.
